SEATTLE PUBLIC SCHOOLS EXPLORER — DATA GUIDE
Compiled October 13, 2026

PUBLISH TO GITHUB PAGES
Extract the ZIP. Upload index.html, all four CSV files, attendance_flows.json, and the entire maps folder to the same repository folder. The documentation files can be uploaded too. In repository Settings > Pages, select Deploy from a branch, your branch, and the folder containing index.html. Open the resulting Pages URL. Opening index.html from disk does not permit CSV fetches. Map previews need the maps folder; the OpenStreetMap iframe needs internet access.

DATA FILES
school_profiles.csv: 106 unique school_key rows. Slower-changing directory data and explicitly dated annual/program/facility/finance/assessment snapshots. This is not a claim that every statistic is current in 2026.
p223_enrollment.csv: 2,226 rows. Key is school_key + p223_reporting_month. 21 monthly reports covering September–June 2024–25, September–June 2025–26, and September 2026 (2026–27). Missing separate school reports have blank counts, not zero.
assessment_results.csv: 822 SBA general SBAC grade/subject rows, All Students, 2025–26. All Grades rows overlap grade detail and must not be summed. WA-AIM excluded.
map_results.csv: 14 precisely dated, school-published MAP grade and overall results at Genesee Hill (spring 2025). Not a districtwide MAP dataset. The dashboard no longer shows a MAP section; the file remains for the field reference and export.
attendance_flows.json: October 1, 2025 head counts from the 2025–26 Annual Enrollment Report, Tables 4-A (K–5), 4-C (grades 6–8) and 4-E (grades 9–12). One record per school and grade band. enrolled_from is the attendance area where students at the school live. residents_attend is the school attended by students who live in that attendance area. Option schools have residence flows only. Counts are not a P223 month and do not include preschool. Executive directors were added to school_profiles.csv from the SPS Executive Directors of Schools page retrieved October 16, 2026. Section 10 school 2025/26 race percentages were checked against 72 existing profile rows and matched, so those percentages were not replaced.
source_provenance.json: complete per-school program/facility source evidence, page numbers, caveats, all column definitions, finance data and monthly district reconciliation controls.
seattle_public_schools_sources_and_validation.pdf: readable column-by-column source guide.

HOW THE DASHBOARD WORKS
The page fetches all four CSVs at load and joins school_key. It starts with the alphabetically first school, Adams, and the latest report month. Year selection uses that school year's latest published report; month selection then changes every P223 card and grade table. The trend uses a calendar-month axis shaded by school year; July, August and any unpublished month appear as gaps, never interpolated values. Calculated summary figures: enrollment change compares the selected month with the same month a year earlier; capacity used divides the selected month's K–12 head count by 2024–25 right-sized or operational capacity (different years); attendance-flow shares divide the home-school count by all area residents, or by all enrolled students, for each grade band in the October 1, 2025 table and appear only where the home school is identified; student-service shares divide each 2024–25 OSPI count by the same record's all-students total; projected students per classroom teacher divides the 2026–27 budget's projected K–12 AAFTE by its classroom teacher FTE and is not class size. Demographic and SBA comparison bands and medians use only SPS schools at the same grade level (elementary, K–8, middle or high school, from the school name or grades served); schools without a grade level show no comparison. Annual statistics retain their own reporting years. The HTML embeds definitions and map-label coordinates, not statistical rows.

UPDATING
Append P223 monthly rows using the existing 109 columns and YYYY-MM month. Keep one row per school/month; missing report rows may be blank or omitted. Do not replace blank with zero. New schools need a unique profile school_key. Grade sums, gender sums, K–12 counts and duplicate keys are validated at load. New program/facility/assessment fields require updated column definitions, rather than unannotated extra columns. Update sources, periods and source guide when replacing annual values. Assessment detail files must retain their supplied headers. Changing CSV values refreshes the dashboard without rebuilding it; map label geometry requires updating HTML configuration if the source PDF changes.

KEY DEFINITIONS AND LIMITS
Special education: affirmative service host destinations in official 2026–27 linked-school matrices. Unknown is not No, not absence of IEP support. Models do not supply enrollment or staffing intensity. Pathfinder has conflicting Distinct entries; evidence is preserved.
Afterschool: Yes only where onsite evidence supports it. Several listed community-center providers have unverified campus locations. Health partner associations can be nearby clinics; they are not automatically onsite.
Facilities: 2025 inventory, 2022 Säzän report and 2024–25 capacity charts. The report reflects a 2021 assessment. Historical conditions/capacity/portables do not describe newly opened replacement buildings. Capacity charts may use interim sites. Right-sized capacity and operational capacity use different classroom counts.
Portable counts: physical assessed structures (2022), portable/modular classroom counts (2024–25), and assessed portable condition rows are separate. Several portable IDs repeat in original assessments; those source rows are retained and explained.
Condition scales: building and learning environment 1–5, lower better. Accessibility is a different 0–7 scale. FMP summary differs from original Säzän at Cleveland (3 vs 2), Lincoln (2 vs 3), Maple (4 vs 3); both values remain.
Renovation/project funding: inventory levy does not necessarily fund every date in a row. Verified current project milestones are separate; some projects used state grants, not a levy. Only 16 current milestones were independently confirmed; other rows use dated inventory evidence.
SBA: 2025–26, data as of September 9, 2026. Proficient = Levels 3–4 (consistent grade level). Foundational or above = Levels 2–4, a different threshold. Achievement denominator is tested students; participation denominator is expected students. OSPI changed denominator reporting in September 2026, so older expected-student percentages are not directly comparable. Suppressed counts/percentages remain blank individually. Average scale scores are not available in this dataset.
MAP: 104 school CSIPs reviewed. Only Genesee Hill supplied a precisely dated whole-school reading/math achievement pair verified for this extraction. Targets, growth, projected SBA performance, percentiles and selected-grade/subgroup rates have not been relabeled whole-school achieved proficiency. Additional qualified plan context is supplied where found. The school-published threshold is not defined in the Genesee Hill source.
Finance: Title I/LAP are 2026–27 adopted school allocations. Per-capita funding is allocation / projected K–12 AAFTE in the same budget year, not current P223. Actual per-pupil spending is OSPI 2024–25 school-level finance data using its finance enrollment denominator. Central expenditures may be attributed differently from the SPS school-funded budget. These measures are displayed separately. Interagency's printed budget per student disagrees with budget / projected AAFTE; both values are retained.
Board: directors checked October 13, 2026; joined using each school's existing board district. The location panel opens the 2026–27 elementary and middle school attendance-area map (updated June 11, 2026), the 2026–27 high school attendance-area map (updated June 10, 2026), or the School Board’s citywide director-districts map. That map is the SharePoint file “SPS Director Districts Map.pdf” (PDF title “SPS Director Districts - 2026/27”, file modified August 18, 2026; the printed disclaimer names MapFile SPS_District_2024). The public opener is the link on Meet the Board, retrieved October 17, 2026. The June 10, 2026 all-schools map remains an alternate and has no attendance-area or director boundary overlay. Red outlines indicate printed labels, not attendance-area polygons, campus footprints, or director-district boundaries. Some schools are not printed on a map; the original PDF remains linked. Middle College has no label on the citywide director map. A second “Roxhill” label near Chief Sealth is not boxed; the highlighted label is “Roxhill at E.C. Hughes.”
Maps: OpenStreetMap is a street map, not photographic street-view imagery. Coordinates use verified city school-location points. No pin is guessed for unresolved locations or multi-site programs.

VALIDATION
Every monthly school table reconciled grade measures, gender and K–12 totals; district totals reconciled against school records plus excluded reporting units. All 21 sources retained. FTE tolerance accounts for source rounding (0.08); integer counts exact. No source grade discrepancies found.
Profile joins checked for all 106 stable keys. Facility capacity charts visually reviewed from raster tables; score cells verified against original reports. Published budgets checked against projected enrollment and independently calculated per-capita figures. OSPI expenditures reconcile total/enrollment to per-pupil values. Released SBA percentages reconcile source counts and valid ranges; suppression is preserved.
All four CSV exports round-trip verified cell by cell with Artifact Tool. Dashboard logic tested across all 106 schools and three years, selectors, trend measures, map markup, CSV download, missing files, invalid schemas, duplicate school/month keys and local-file loading errors. Browser visual QA was not available in this execution environment; live map loading depends on host/network availability.
